# Licences of the NEXUS fixtures taken from other projects

The `nexus-*.json` files in this folder hold small NEXUS inputs taken from, or inspired by, the test suites of other libraries.
Every fixture says where it came from (`source`), under which licence (`licence`) and whether the input was copied
(`copied: "verbatim"`, abridged where the note says so) or only the idea was taken and the input written from scratch
(`copied: "rephrased"`). Inputs from GPL, LGPL and Artistic-licensed projects (forester, `ape`, `treeio`) are never copied; they
are `rephrased`. The verbatim ones come from the three permissively licensed projects below, whose notices are kept here as their
licences require.
