tip label = protein stable id = FASTA id = members.tsv `protein_id`

## Quirks

Murinae collection: species are mouse/rat strains (production names like mus_musculus_c57bl6nj) which do NOT match the GBIF backbone as species; collapse to Mus musculus/Rattus norvegicus before matching. Many paralogs per species.
